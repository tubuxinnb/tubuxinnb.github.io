I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Pittsburgh (**Pitt**), advised by **Prof. Xulong Tang**.
My current research focuses on LLM systems, especially **agentic serving systems** and **context engineering**.

Before that, I received my M.S. from the University of Science and Technology of China (**USTC**), where I was advised by **Prof. Hong An**.
My research there focused on computer architecture, including Hardware/Software co-design for HPC workloads and memory systems.
In particular, I worked on near-data processing systems and address translation mechanisms, including address translation designs for NDP architectures and NDP-based co-designs for accelerating density functional theory workloads.
